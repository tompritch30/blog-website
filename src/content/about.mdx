I build LLM systems that run in production, first at a law firm and now for wealth managers.

I started in biology. I studied Biological Sciences at Imperial College London and graduated in 2021 with a First, having made the Dean's List in my first and second years.

I then moved into computing and went back to Imperial for an MSc in Computer Science, which I finished with a Distinction in 2024. I'm co-first author of [ForestVO: Enhancing Visual Odometry in Forest Environments through ForestGlue](https://doi.org/10.1109/lra.2025.3557738), published in IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters in 2025. Visual odometry works out how a camera moves from the images it records. Our method keeps baseline accuracy while using 25% of the keypoints, the image features it tracks from frame to frame.

In October 2024 I joined Fletchers Solicitors, a personal-injury and medical-negligence law firm, as a data scientist. I built a WhatsApp agent that answers questions from leads the firm had written off and books them a call; it won back 10% of them. I also built an Azure transcription service with a custom legal glossary to free up legal review time, and ran the LLM analysis on 5,000+ recorded calls behind a case for the firm's C-suite.

In April 2026 I joined Palindrome, an AI platform for UK wealth managers, as its first Forward Deployed Engineer. My job is to get the platform working inside client firms: scoping, pricing, proof of concept, production and support. Much of it is learning how a firm's advisers write their advice reports and building the AI agents that draft them. In my first 3 months I shipped 5 AI advice-report products to production for two wealth managers, and adviser report writing went from a week to an hour. I also represent Palindrome in the FCA's AI Live Testing programme, where we refined our evaluation system in workshops with the regulator.

Outside work, I'm an Airbnb Superhost, which I still do each week. In 2015 I was a ball boy at Wimbledon, and I've volunteered in Malawi and Cambodia.
