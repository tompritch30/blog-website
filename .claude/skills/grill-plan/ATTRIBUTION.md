Adapted: 2026-09-24. Kept: design tree, frontier asked in one numbered round, a recommended answer per question, facts looked up by the asker and never asked, docs (CONTEXT.md, ADRs) updated as decisions land. Changed: the questioner is a fresh read-only agent spawned by the lead and the answerer is the builder, not a human; the griller walks the whole tree but asks one round only, with conditional answers for downstream nodes, then the plan is decided (the lead rules on rejected blockers); each question cites the doc it rests on and is classed blocker, should or owner; owner-class questions are parked in the plan file's Log, built on a logged default when reversible and stopped when not.
Why the change: upstream says planning is human-in-the-loop (https://www.aihero.dev/things-people-get-wrong-with-grill-me-and-grill-with-docs). When the owner is not in the loop, the docs the owner settled stand in for the owner, and anything they do not settle is parked for the owner rather than guessed. With the owner present, use `grilling` instead.
Copied into blog-website: 2026-10-03 from the Hazel adaptation.
Local note: the plan lives under `plans/`, its Log section at the bottom records the questions and decisions, and the docs the griller checks are `CLAUDE.md`, `CONTEXT.md` and `docs/adr/`. This blog has no issue tracker, milestones or architecture file.
